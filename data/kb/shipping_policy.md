# Shipping Policy

- **Standard shipping:** 5-7 business days (free on orders over $50).
- **Expedited shipping:** 2 business days, normally $15.
- Tracking numbers are emailed within 24 hours of dispatch.

## Wrong or missing items

If a customer received the **wrong item** or an item is **missing** from their
order, agents may arrange a **free replacement** shipped **expedited at no
charge**. This does not require manager approval.

## Delays

If an order is past its expected delivery window, agents may re-send the
tracking details and, for time-sensitive cases, upgrade the reshipment to
expedited at no charge.
