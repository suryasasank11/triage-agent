# Returns & Exchanges

- **Size or color exchanges** are **free** within **30 days** of delivery.
  Agents may arrange these directly; no approval needed.
- Items must be unused and in original packaging.
- We provide a prepaid return label for all exchanges.
- Exchanges ship via standard shipping unless the customer has a documented
  deadline, in which case expedited may be offered at no charge.

Exchanges are **not** refunds. Swapping an item for a different size, color, or
model is always handled directly by the agent.
