# Warranty

- All Harbor Goods products carry a **1-year limited warranty** covering
  manufacturing defects (broken zippers, seam failure, hardware faults).
- Warranty claims for a defective item are resolved with a **free replacement**
  of the same item. Agents may arrange this directly.
- Damage from normal wear, misuse, or accidents is **not** covered.
- If a customer reports a **health or safety issue** (injury, allergic
  reaction, product hazard), do **not** attempt to resolve it. Escalate to a
  human immediately.
