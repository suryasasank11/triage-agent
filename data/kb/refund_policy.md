# Refund Policy

Harbor Goods issues refunds only under the conditions below.

- Refunds require **manager approval**. Support agents are **not authorized** to
  promise, confirm, or issue a refund directly. Any request for a refund must be
  **escalated to a human** for review.
- Eligible orders: unused items returned within **14 days** of delivery.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-10 business days
  **after** approval.
- Shipping charges are non-refundable except where an item arrived damaged or
  incorrect.

Do not quote a specific refund amount or timeline to the customer until a human
has approved the request.
